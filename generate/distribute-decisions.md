A clothing brand is looking automate the way they choose to replace items for their store locations. Every store is expected to have shirts and pants in equal number. They use the letter *d* to denote the difference in quantities of tops (positive) and bottoms (negative). At the end of the day, every store requests *d* replacement items. The brand's distributor looks at the orders and attempts to ship a pair of new items so there is no item left over.
